Introduction to Resident Evil

Resident Evil is a popular survival horror video game franchise developed by Capcom and first released in 1996. The series is set in a world threatened by deadly viruses, zombies, and terrifying mutated creatures. Players follow different protagonists as they fight to survive, investigate mysterious outbreaks, and uncover the secrets behind the organizations responsible for them. With its dark atmosphere, suspenseful gameplay, memorable characters, and iconic locations such as Raccoon City, Resident Evil has become one of the most influential franchises in the survival horror genre.

  • Chris Redfield

    Skilled BSAA agent & determined protagonist fighting bioterrorism.

  • Leon S. Kennedy

    Rookie cop turned elite agent — brave, quick-witted, and always saves the day.

  • Jill Valentine

    Brave S.T.A.R.S. member — strong, smart, and a true survivor from the very beginning.

  • Ada Wong

    Mysterious spy with hidden agendas & complex alliances.

Our Research Journey

Through this research journey, our group researched what players struggle with in the Resident Evil game — from managing limited ammos, healing items, solving tricky puzzles, navigating dark and confusing hallways, and also defeating bosses. We learned players struggle with the story, characters, supplies, and puzzles — so we made a website to share simple tips and strategies so that anyone can enjoy playing the game without getting frustrated and lost.
